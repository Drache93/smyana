# Credits

**Смяна (Smyana)** was made for Game Off 2026 (theme: Shift).

## Who made it

- **Direction, design and every decision that mattered:** Dominic Cassidy. His voice notes and messages are kept word
  for word in [`archive/notes/`](archive/notes/), and what he decided, and when, is in
  [`archive/decisions.md`](archive/decisions.md).
- **Code, art, sound and music:** written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, working to that direction. This
  is the game's AI disclosure: nearly all of the code, the voxel art, the synthesised sound and the score were
  produced by the AI and judged, redirected or rejected by Dominic. The [`archive/`](archive/) shows the whole
  exchange: pictures, bugs, dead ends, journals.
- **First-time players:** AI agents that played builds cold from screenshots; their reports are in
  [`archive/playtests/`](archive/playtests/).

## What it is built on

- **Creel**, the engine, by Casaide (Apache License 2.0): <https://github.com/Casaide/creel>. First built for *Age of
  Anything*.
- **Lobster**, the language Creel and the game are written in, by Wouter van Oortmerssen (Apache License 2.0):
  <https://github.com/aardappel/lobster>, in the fork <https://github.com/Drache93/lobster>.
- **Old Standard TT**, the typeface, by Alexey Kryukov (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

## Sound and music

Every sound and every track is synthesised by the scripts in [`sound/`](sound/) and [`music/`](music/). No recordings,
samples or libraries are used. The tunes are original, written in the manner of Rhodope music from the sources in
[`docs/rhodope-music-style.md`](docs/rhodope-music-style.md).

## The place

The village is made up. Its model is the old stone villages of the Western Rhodopes, Kovachevitsa above all. The
sources for the trees, the game animals, the houses and the rest are named where they are used, in
[`docs/`](docs/) and in the code.
