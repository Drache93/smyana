SMYANA PLAYTEST EIGHT: first-time player report

I reached the third place with all 20 men alive, in 93 steps and 32:06 of game clock. The game was still running when
I stopped it. One caveat on evidence: my own view of pictures 0001 to 0043 was dropped part-way through the session,
so for the first 13 minutes I rely on the log and my notes from the time; pictures 0044 to 0093 I can vouch for
directly.

1. WHAT I THINK IT IS, AND WHERE I WOULD HAVE QUIT
You lead twenty riflemen on foot out of a Bulgarian mountain village, "out over the ridge, and home by spring"
(Stoyan, 0:11). You walk east from place to place while day, night and seasons turn, feed the men by picking and
hunting, and camp by a fire at night. It is very good-looking and calm. In 32 minutes nothing threatened me.

Quit moments:
- 12:30 to 15:30, beech valley: six steps of right-clicking the screen edge with nothing happening (0043 to 0050).
- 16:11 to 17:45: the camp button did nothing twice and the screen went almost black (0054 to 0057). This was the
  worst moment.
- 25:00 to 27:50: off the track after avoiding the zaptiye, with no idea whether I was going the right way (0073 to
  0079).

3. DECISIONS, AND THINGS THAT APPEARED
Real decisions:
- Zaptiye (20:44) was the only clear one. I understood both options and went round. I never saw the zaptiye on screen
  (0065, 0066), so I could not judge how wide to go or whether I had passed them. Nothing confirmed I had.
- Camp now or push on at dusk. I understood it. Pushing on at 10:23 walked me into the next place in the dark with no
  cost I could see.
- Food versus distance was a pressure I felt only from the loaves dropping from 4 to 2 between 24:35 and 30:05. Nobody
  said a word about food running low.

Appearances:
- Strawberries (0:02): a man called them out. I walked that way, did not find them, and gave up.
- Roe deer (3:26): I spotted them myself at the screen edge and right-clicked. Eight shots, one down. Angel's line on
  how to hunt ("come up slow and stop short") arrived at 3:31, one second after the volley, which is too late. "Send
  men to it with the knife" was clear and worked.
- "They are away" came three times (2:23, 4:21, 30:36). I never saw the animals in question. The 4:21 one came while
  the men were sitting at camp.
- Red-marked plants (6:43): the basket pointer and the "gather" sounds worked, and Iliya's "A few handfuls more in the
  packs" was good feedback.
- Hips: "hard and green inside yet. Come the autumn" (3:01, 7:21) is a nice line. In autumn I got "The birds have had
  the best of this one" three times (12:05, 21:16, 24:50). I never found a bush that paid out in autumn.
- Stone hut (0051 to 0059): the pointer became a flame ("camp here"). I right-clicked it, and nothing visible happened
  in or at the hut.
- Dark animal in the fir woods (0089, 0090): I mistook it for a boulder until it moved. Shot it. Nobody said what it
  was.
- A red upright shape stands near where I arrived in the fir woods (0080 to 0084), and more like it further on (0089,
  0093). I never learned what they are (a red bush, a scarecrow, a figure?).

4. FINDING MY WAY
- Village edge: Kostadin's line, a "The way on" label with a ring at the right edge, and the visible lane together
  worked. Atanas's dawn line, "East again, down the lane" (6:26), helped too. I was never lost; it took about ten
  minutes including a night.
- Beech valley: "The track goes on across this place. That way." was spoken at 10:36 in the dark, and "that way"
  pointed at nothing I could see. The next morning there was no marker in any picture from 0044 to 0079. I followed
  the green track by eye. That worked until the zaptiye detour, after which I was lost from roughly 24:50 to 28:01 and
  left the place by luck.
- Fir woods: the "The way on" ring was back, bottom right, on arrival (0080, 0081). It was gone after the night (0083,
  0084). I walked towards where it had been.
- Plainly: the ring helps when it is there, but it vanishes when you most need it, which is the morning after and
  after a detour.

5. CONFUSIONS
- The camp button failed at 16:11 and 17:04, and so did right-clicking the hut with the flame pointer at 17:25. It
  worked at 17:45 only once the night was nearly over. The men said "Make a fire here" at 16:57 while I was trying to.
  My guess is that the men had been deselected or were still walking, but the screen told me nothing.
- The selection is lost after every camp, so I had to drag a box again each morning.
- The loaves are tiny and unexplained. They turn into hollow outlines (0089), then came back as three after
  butchering. I never learned what one loaf means.
- The hour jumped from 14h to 21h on entering the fir woods and from 18h to 0h entering the beech valley. Both
  arrivals were at night, which felt like a penalty I had not earned.
- Summer became autumn during one night (11:13).
- In the fir woods the trees are tall bare black poles across the whole view (0083 to 0093). The men are hard to see
  behind them, and I could not tell whether this is meant to be dead forest or the tops are being hidden.
- "No way through there" (31:32) was said while the men walked to the animal; I saw no obstacle.

6. SOUND AND MUSIC
- What fitted: the reload sequence after the volley; the humming fire track at night; a far wolf howl at 5:14 answered
  by "They keep clear of a fire"; the owl; stream and wading sounds at the fords; and the bagpipe dusk track arriving
  with the hut.
- Silences: music stops for long stretches each mid-day (about 2:01 to 3:50, 8:11 to 10:14, 13:56 to 15:36, 20:07 to
  21:57, 26:31 to 28:03). With the ambient beds these were acceptable, though the afternoons felt empty.
- What did not fit: 10_wary started on entering the fir woods at 28:03 and ran through a quiet night's sleep with
  nothing dangerous in sight, and the "danger is over" stroke only came at 31:58, after I had butchered an animal. I
  never knew what the danger was. No music change went with the zaptiye at all.
- Rain and birdsong swap every few seconds at times (19:14 to 19:25, 26:38 to 26:45, 30:23 to 30:34), which would
  sound twitchy.
- The summer dusk track played at night in the beech valley (10:33).

8. START AND BETWEEN-PLACE SCREENS
- The menu had menu music and a Start button; one click and I was in the forest within two seconds. I saw no story or
  control explanation; the men's first lines carried it, just about. I learned drag-select and right-click by guessing
  from other games. The pointer changing (ring, basket, sight, knife, flame) is the best teaching in the game.
- Between places, the log shows "leaving" then "arriving" lasting two to three seconds each time. I never saw a
  picture of either and nothing told me the name of the new place on screen that I noticed; I only know the place
  names from the log. No letter or text page ever appeared.

9. TEN CHANGES, IN ORDER
1. Keep "The way on" visible (or bring it back when the men stand still, at dawn, and after a detour).
2. Make the camp button always do something, or say why it cannot.
3. Lighten the night, or keep the men and icons readable in it.
4. Do not arrive in a new place at night; or show the hours passing on the between-places screen.
5. Show the zaptiye, or at least a direction, and tell me when I am past them.
6. Have a man speak when food is short, and explain the loaves once.
7. Say the hunting advice when deer are first seen, not after the shot; stop "They are away" for animals the player
never saw.
8. Show the place name on arrival.
9. Fir woods: thin or fade the black trunks in front of the men; fix the slowdown.
10. Tie the wary music to something visible; cut the repeated "birds have had the best" line or let some autumn bushes
pay.
