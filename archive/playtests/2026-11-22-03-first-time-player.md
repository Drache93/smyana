# Playtest report 3: first-time player, 18:15 of game clock, 110 steps

Played by an agent on 2026-11-22 (night) on the build with the first clarity fixes (D-144), from pictures and ordinary mouse and keyboard input only. The run was cut short at 18:15 of a planned 40 minutes: a usage limit paused the agent and the game process had gone when it came back (no crash line in the log). It covers three game days and two nights.

## 1. What I think it is, and when I would have quit

It is a slow real-time journey game. You lead a company of 20 Bulgarian riflemen out of their village along a forest lane, keep them fed by hunting and gathering, camp each night, and (from one line at 0:12, "A long road. Out over the ridge, and home by spring") get them over a ridge and home again by spring. I am about 60% sure of the goal, because it rests on that single line and nothing ever repeats it or shows progress. After 18 minutes the place name was still "The village edge", the season was still summer, and nothing had threatened me. I would have quit for good at about 11:00, on the second night: four men at my fire, 16 lost in the dark, no way to call them back, and no sign I had moved forward at all. Playing on to 18:15 did not make it better; it was more walking, darkness and hunger complaints I could not act on.

## 2. The first five minutes

- **0:00 to 1:00.** The menu is the Cyrillic title over 20 men in a wood, with Start and Quit (0001). It looks lovely. Start drops you straight in with no intro text. At 0:05 Stoyan says "Which way? The lane runs east, down to the beeches", and a hint says to drag across the men. I dragged; faint rings appeared, plus a small bar of icons (fire, sack, hand, four loaves) floating by the men (0003). Hovering gave "Make camp" and "Halt"; the loaves have no tooltip. The pointer said a right click sends the men, so I right-clicked to the right. They walked to that spot and stopped; the camera did not follow (0009). A right click at the screen edge got "No way through there" (0:36). I guessed the arrow keys and they scroll.
- **1:00 to 2:00.** Rain started, which looked good. I sent the men to a berry bush; they walked there and nothing happened, and the bush later vanished with no message. I spent five hovers chasing the sack icon, because the bar slides as the men shuffle (0016 to 0020). I finally clicked it and the loaves shrank from four to one: I had dropped the packs without ever seeing a tooltip. A "pick these up" pointer let me undo it. By 1:30 it was already 10h.
- **2:00 to 3:00.** I walked the lane. The music ended at 2:09 and restarted a second later. I saw a fruit tree in a sunny meadow off the lane and right-clicked it: "No way through there" and the men stopped. The grass beside it gave the same, and so did the next click along what I thought was the lane (2:14, 2:24, 2:34). I was lost. I scrolled about and found a worn track going down-right. So "east" is screen lower-right, which nothing tells you.
- **3:00 to 4:00.** The best minute. A deer appeared (0035), the pointer turned to a gun sight, and my right click made the whole company fire a volley with smoke (0037). The pointer became a knife; I right-clicked and they butchered it, and the loaves went from four to five with no message or sound. Angel shouted "Strawberries!". I found a basket pointer over tiny red dots, right-clicked, and three men went to pick (0041). At 3:53 Ivan said "The light is going. We should make camp."
- **4:00 to 5:00.** I clicked Make camp. A tripod and fire appeared, and the dusk fiddle started as the fire lit (4:17), which was lovely. After that there was nothing to do. The men stood at attention round the fire in the dark (0045).

## 3. Controls

Found:
- **Drag to select:** from the on-screen hint.
- **Right click to move:** from the pointer text.
- **Arrow keys scroll:** a guess, after the screen edge did nothing. WASD seems to scroll too.
- **Right click on a deer shoots, then butchers; right click on strawberries gathers:** from the pointer changing to a sight, a knife and a basket. This is the best teaching in the game.
- **Make camp, Put the fire out, Halt:** from icon tooltips.
- **Sack icon drops the packs:** by accident.
- **Space pauses:** a guess.
- **Click one man:** shows his name and "well" at bottom left, the only status readout I ever found.

Never understood:
- What the loaves count (days, or loaves each), and why men starve while four are showing.
- Why you would drop packs.
- Which red-fruited plants are food. The big bushes and the fruit tree give a plain move pointer; only the tiny ground dots can be gathered.
- How to select everyone, or find men who are off screen.
- Whether the stone hut does anything.
- How far I had to go, or what cold and danger are.
- Whether the night can be skipped.

## 4. Lost or stuck

- **2:14 to 2:40.** Three "No way through there" in a row on open meadow and near the lane (0030 to 0032). Walkable ground looks the same as blocked ground. A different pointer over blocked ground would have fixed it.
- **6:46 to 8:50.** The stone hut showed a flame pointer, "camp here". I right-clicked it and nothing came. Then 13 selected men stood frozen for about 70 seconds through five move orders with no words, while unselected men wandered (0060 to 0067). They only moved after I pressed Halt and ordered again.
- **8:57 to 10:30.** A right click meant as a move landed on a gather spot; the pointer had flicked to a basket. The men scattered singly over about three screens, the icon bar floated over empty ground, and I could not find them (0073 to 0080). A double click lost my selection. There is no select-all, no map and no roster.
- **10:37.** On the hut, the pointer says "camp here"; I right-click and get "No way through there" (0084). The game offers a camp and then refuses it.
- **13:20.** Clicking "Put the fire out" deselected the men, so my next move order went nowhere.
- **13:30 to 16:00.** Four men took about two and a half minutes to walk round the stream to a lane I could see just across it. I followed the icon bar with the arrow keys under solid tree canopy (0100).

## 5. Pace

- **Days are far too short for the walking speed.** A game hour is about 15 to 22 seconds, so daylight lasts about three and a half minutes. I covered two or three screens a day. Three days in, the place name had not changed.
- **Nights are about two minutes of watching.** Night one ran from 4:17 to 6:15, night two from 10:50 to 13:00. Time does speed up while they sleep, but it is still dead time with no skip.
- **The camera not following is constant busywork.** Every move order is a click, a scroll, and another click.
- **Nothing happened to me in 18 minutes apart from one deer.** No wolves, bandits or cold. The only pressures were the dark and the hunger lines.
- **Nothing was too fast,** except the sliding icon bar.

## 6. Sound and music

- **Fits:** the summer day track over the walking. The dusk fiddle starting as the first fire lit at 4:17 is the best cue in the game.
- **Wrong moment:** 19_the_fire, the men humming, started at 5:57 and at 11:29, when the men were lying asleep. It should play in the evening while they stand at the fire.
- **Repetition:** 01_summer_day replayed at least six times in 18 minutes (0:02, 2:10, 7:43, 9:22, 13:15, 15:22).
- **Mismatch:** walking in pitch dark at 17:29 the track was the warm "summer dusk". There is no wary or night track when you travel at night.
- **The volley:** 17 pan sounds and 17 booms in the same second at 3:12, then about 18 seconds of 17 men's reload sounds (cock, pour, rod) in bunches. That is probably a wall of noise.
- **Missing:** any sound for butchering, gathering, food gained, "No way through", the hunger warnings, the fire lighting, rain, or the stream. The hunger lines are the most important information in the game and are silent text.

## 7. Enjoyed, and what made me want to stop

Enjoyed:
- The look: rain, dusk light, the fire glow at night (0044 to 0046).
- The deer volley and the pointer changing to sight, then knife.
- "Strawberries!" being called out by a man.
- The men lying down with one sitting watch.
- The ring marker at the screen edge pointing to off-screen men.

Wanted to stop:
- Orders silently ignored.
- Men scattering with no way to gather them.
- "No way through there" on ground that looks open.
- Chasing a moving icon bar.
- Two-minute nights.
- No sense of progress or of any goal.

## 8. Leaving the path; hunger, cold, night, danger, food

- **Leaving the path.** I had reasons: the fruit tree, the hut, the strawberries, the deer. But the game punished it. The tree and the hut were "No way through", and leaving the path for strawberries is how my company fell apart.
- **Hunger.** I half understood it. The loaves went from five to four overnight with no message. From 11:33 the men said "My belly is empty" and "I can't go much further without food" while the icon still showed four loaves (0110). I could not tell whether this was real, whose food it was, or what would happen. The first warnings came at night, when I could do nothing.
- **Getting food.** I learned shooting, butchering and strawberries from the pointer, which works. Neither the berry bushes nor the strawberries ever visibly added a loaf.
- **Cold.** Never appeared.
- **Night.** I understood "make camp", but only because Ivan said it. Walking on at night had no consequence other than a nearly black screen (0109).
- **Danger.** None in 18 minutes.

## 9. Ten changes, most important first

1. **Make select-all trivial.** A key or double click that selects the whole company, and a count of how many are selected. Better, keep the company as one body by default.
2. **Make the camera follow the selected men,** or give a "centre on men" key, and say on screen that arrow keys scroll.
3. **Show blocked ground before the click:** a "can't go" pointer over blocked ground, and no hard stop when a click is refused. Fix the meadow and fruit tree that look reachable but are not.
4. **Fix the hut.** "Camp here" followed by "No way through" is a contradiction, and the first hut order was ignored without a word.
5. **Show the goal and progress.** State the goal at the start and keep a small indicator of distance to the ridge. Change the place name sooner than 18 minutes in.
6. **Explain food.** Add a tooltip on the loaves, a visible "+food" when gathering or butchering, and make the hunger warnings match the loaves. Do not show four loaves beside "I can't go much further without food".
7. **Lengthen the day or speed the walking,** and let the player skip the night once the men are asleep.
8. **Pin the icon bar to a fixed place on screen.** A bar that moves under the pointer caused my accidental pack drop.
9. **Stop gathering from scattering the company.** Send a few men and keep the rest together, as the first strawberry patch did, and bring the pickers back by themselves.
10. **Put something in the first ten minutes:** a far howl at night, a stranger on the road, anything. And mark edible plants so they read differently from decoration.

## 10. How far I got, and likely bugs

At 18:15 it was summer, 22h of day three, at "The village edge", with 20 men alive. Four were with me on a second lane beyond the stream; the other 16 were somewhere near the hut and I never found them.

Likely bugs:
- **Frozen men:** 13 selected men ignored five right-click move orders over about 70 seconds, with no speech, until I pressed Halt (0060 to 0067). The first right click on the hut with the flame pointer also did nothing (0053 to 0055).
- **Hut:** flame pointer "camp here", then "No way through there" (0083, 0084).
- **Icon bar over empty ground:** it sat where no men were while they were scattered or off screen (0073 to 0075).
- **"Put the fire out" clears the selection** (0094, 0095).
- **Fire with almost no one at it:** Make camp with four men selected lit a fire that the other 16 never came to (0085, 0086).
- **Hunger lines against the loaves:** men said they were starving with four loaves shown (0110).
- **Fire humming track plays while everyone sleeps** (5:57, 11:29).
- **Berry bush:** it disappeared at about 1:15 after the men stood by it, with no gather pointer, no message and no change in food (0014 to 0020).
- **Speech placement:** the text appears at top left of the screen, not over the speaker (0002, 0110), so I could never tell who or where the speaker was.
