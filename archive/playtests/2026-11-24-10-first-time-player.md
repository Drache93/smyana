# Playtest report 10: first-time player, 27:05 of game clock, 85 steps

Played by an agent on 2026-11-24 (afternoon) on the build with D-186 to D-190 (the packs as a woven bag, hunger that
bites, rings on what is called out, the line about four days' bread). It was asked above all whether the game makes a

PLAYTEST REPORT: Smyana, cold first play. 85 steps, game clock 0:00 to 27:05.

A caveat on quotes: the men's lines are copied from the log and are exact. The first 39 pictures dropped out of my
view during the session, so ring labels and screen positions from 0:00 to about 8:20 are from memory and marked
"(memory)". Everything after 8:20 is quoted from pictures I can still see.

## 1. What I think the game is, and where I would have quit

Twenty armed men leave a mountain village in summer on a long walk east, "Out over the ridge, and home by spring." You
box-select them and right click to move, from one place to the next (The village edge, The beech valley, The deep fir
woods). Bread in the packs runs down, and you top it up by shooting deer, picking fruit and taking caches. You light a
fire at dusk and sleep. Seasons turn as you go, and Ottoman police (zaptiye) and wolves are the dangers. I never
learned why the men are travelling or what they carry the hides and wool for.

- **Likeliest quit, 23:30 to 27:05 (The deep fir woods).** "The way on" ring disappeared, the track was invisible
  under black tree trunks, and I wandered for three and a half minutes with no direction, into dusk. Nothing told me
  where to go.
- **Second quit point, 16:57 to 18:24 (zaptiye attack on the sleeping camp).** I had done what the game asked ("The
  light is going. We should make camp."). Eight seconds after I clicked the fire, the warning about zaptiye came. The
  camp was then shot at for over a minute, two men died, and nothing woke the men or handed me control. It read as
  punishment for obeying.
- **Mild quit risk, 8:30 to 9:13 and 12:00 to 15:30.** Long stretches of only walking, about 20 seconds per
  screen-width, with nothing said.

The first four minutes are the strongest part: bread warning, cache, deer, strawberries, hut, camp, all prompted and
all working.

## 4. Bugs and rough edges, worst first

1. **A sleeping camp does not react to an attack.** From 17:11 to 18:18 the zaptiye fired about fifteen shots into
   the camp. The men stood around the fire and one died before I intervened. No line said "we are attacked", and the
   view did not move to show the shooters; they happened to be at the bottom edge of picture 0059.
2. **Zaptiye warning arrives after the camp order.** "We should make camp." at 15:48, fire clicked, then "Zaptiye on
   the road ahead, resting." at 15:57. The men lit a fire within sight of them. The advice "Off the road, into the
   trees, and keep still." came while the men were asleep and could not be followed.
3. **"The way on" ring vanishes.** It seems to show only when the men are near the track or have just arrived. It is
   most needed when you have strayed, and that is exactly when it is gone (deep fir woods, 23:34 to 27:05).
4. **The deep fir woods are unreadable.** Black bare trunks stripe the whole screen (pictures 0072 to 0085), with
   men, animals and track hidden behind them. At night (0070, 0071) the screen is near black outside the fire. I could
   not find the track at all.
5. **Time jumps between places.** Left the village edge at 13h and arrived at 17h dusk; left the beech valley at 14h
   and arrived at 20h night. Each arrival is immediately "make camp", so a day is: wake, walk two minutes, sleep.
   Twice I arrived and camped within 15 seconds.

8. **"They are away. We came on them too fast." followed by a kill.** (7:59, then 8:02.)
9. **Split party and misplaced icon bar.** At 26:13 (picture 0083) the men were in two groups at opposite sides of

## 5. The three changes that would help a newcomer most

1. **Keep "The way on" on screen always,** pinned at the screen edge in the direction of the exit however far the men
   stray, with the label on one fixed side. Make the track visible through the fir trunks, or thin and fade the trunks
   near the men.
2. **Make danger at camp playable.** Do not offer "We should make camp." when zaptiye are within reach, or say the
   zaptiye line first. When a camp is fired on, wake the men, say so in a line, and turn the view to the attackers.
3. **Keep the first place's density going, and say what things are for.** Later places need a food prompt every minute
   or two as the village edge has. Add one line each for what a hut does (and a visible result when the men reach it),
   what the cache gave, why hides and wool matter, and why one would hide packs under a tree. Drop or vary second uses
   of the same line.
