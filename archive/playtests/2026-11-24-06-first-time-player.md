# Playtest report 6: first-time player, 28:05 of game clock, 130 steps

Played by an agent on 2026-11-24 (morning) on the build with D-163 to D-172 (roe deer by the trail, the certain first
patrol, orders that snap to ground the men can reach, the village's houses and smoke, the loaves' word, the walk's
words under the letter). The agent's own words follow, unchanged but for line breaks.

I reached two places, not three, and 28 minutes, not 30. I spent the last twenty minutes lost in the second place with
nothing happening.

## 1. What I think the game is, and where I would have quit

Twenty Bulgarian men with rifles leave their village in summer to walk "out over the ridge, and home by spring"
(Dimitar, 0:14). I drive them as one group through painted forest, camp at night by a fire, and the bread runs down. It
looks lovely: the autumn beech wood, rain, firelight (pictures 72, 87, 96) are the best thing in it. As a game, in 28
minutes nothing threatened me, nothing was gained, and I never knew where I was meant to go.

Moments I would have quit:

- About 9:00 (pictures 60 to 63). I found a stone hut, the only man-made thing in the valley, and sent the men to it
  three times. Each time: "No way through there." Nothing else. The one interesting thing gave nothing.
- About 13:00 to 16:00. The third day of walking east through trees with no event, no animal, no find. The same line
  about the birds and the bushes for the third time.
- About 19:30 to 22:00 (pictures 100 to 112). A wall of rock all along the east, food at one loaf, no hint of the way
  out. I ended in a dark corner of thick wood at dusk. This is where I would really have stopped.
- 22:34, "That is the last of the bread", then "No food left" (picture 117), and nothing I could do about it.

## 3. Decisions, and things that appeared

Real decisions:

- Which way to go at the start. Understood, because Hristo told me. The only time the game told me a direction.
- When and where to camp. Understood: the men say the light is going, and the flame icon says "Make camp". I never
  learnt whether the spot matters.
- "Set the packs down" (sack icon). I did not understand what it is for or what it costs, so I never pressed it. On
  day five the icon had gone (picture 116) and I do not know why.
- Whether to turn back when the food ran low. I had no information to decide with: no map, no sense of distance, no
  word from the men.

Things that appeared:

- Rose hips, 1:00. A man spoke as we passed. Nothing to do; "come the autumn" suggested I might come back, but I never
  saw a way to gather anything.
- Strawberries, 1:58. Todor shouted. I could not see them or find anything to click. I do not know if the men ate any
  or whether the food went up.
- Wolves, 4:19 and 4:29. Heard only, far off. Never seen in 28 minutes.
- The stone hut, 8:20. Seen on screen. The pointer turned into a flame ("camp here") over it. Could not enter; no line
  about it.
- Picked-over bushes, four times (6:55, 12:01, 18:16, 24:52). Always the same line, always fired by an order to walk,
  never by anything I could see.
- No people and no animals were seen at all.

## 4. What confused me

- Nothing on screen says left-drag selects and right-click moves. I guessed from other games. The harness log told me
  the pointer shows "an arrow with a ring (a right click sends the men here)", but that pointer never showed in a
  screenshot, so I cannot say whether a real player reads it that way.
- The icons sit under the men and move with them. When the men are near the edge the icons are half off screen and the
  tooltip is cut: "Food fo" (picture 97), and a man's words cut to one letter at the right edge (pictures 81, 98, 99).
- The loaves. I only learnt they meant days of food by hovering at 13:30. Three loaves, then two, then one, then a
  grey blob.
- The season changing from summer to autumn overnight on the first night, with no words. I took the autumn trees for
  the new place's look, not time passing.
- The move between places took about three seconds. I did not register it as a screen.
- "No way through there" about a hut. I wanted to go to it, not through it.
- Which way is on. After the first line nobody ever says. There is no map, compass, path or signpost in the beech
  valley. The lane in the first place was the only guide I ever had.
- Rocks: some can be walked through (26:46, by the stream) and some cannot, and they look the same.
- The men sometimes walk somewhere other than where I clicked without saying so (20:58: I clicked south-east and they
  ended up north of where they were, picture 108).
- Tab centres the view on the men. I found that by trying keys; nothing says so. Without it I lost them off screen
  constantly, because the view does not follow them (pictures 73 to 76, 84 to 86).
- What having no food does. The men said the bread was gone and then walked a full day unchanged.

## 5. Sound and music (from the log)

Fitted:

- The fire: flint, crackle coming up, then the men humming (4:19 to 4:27).
- The far howl and Nikola's line together.
- Wading as they crossed the stream; the stream fading in and out as we neared and left it.
- Crickets at dusk, owl at night, jay in autumn, woodpecker in summer.
- The slow bagpipe at dusk twice (9:11, 15:33), right for the light.

Did not fit:

- Rain and birdsong swap every few seconds at times (19:14, 19:22, 19:24; 26:38, 26:44, 26:45). That will sound like
  flickering.
- On the first night the log names the fire track "autumn night, by the fire" at 4:27, three seconds before the clock
  says autumn.
- On the second and third nights the fire humming did not play (fire at 10:32 and 17:15, music ended a few seconds
  later, then silence). On the fourth it did (22:55).
- "ack" on every single order. I heard it about sixty times.

Long silences with no music: 1:56 to 3:48 (nearly two minutes on the first walk), 7:24 to 9:11, 10:39 to 11:45, 13:41
to 15:33, 17:15 to 18:09, 20:05 to 21:57. About a third of the game.

No gunshot, no danger music, no animal near. Not one of the tracks 10 to 18 played.

## 7. The start, and the screen between places

The menu shows the game's title over the live village edge with the men walking past behind it, with the wind and
birdsong and the menu music. It is a good first picture and I understood it: these are the men, this is home. It gives
no story and no controls, and Start needs two clicks.

Between places: "leaving" then "arriving" for about a second each (3:45 to 3:48). I did not see a letter screen. It
went by inside one step and I have no picture of it, so I cannot say what it showed. As a player I only knew I had
moved because the hour jumped from about 17h to 20h and it was dusk. Arriving at dusk meant my first act in the new
place was to camp, without having seen it.

## 8. Ten changes, in order

1. Tell me the way on in every place. A path on the ground, or a man saying "the stream runs out through the rocks to
   the north-east". I lost twenty minutes in the beech valley.
2. Put something in the beech valley. In 24 minutes there: one far howl and a locked hut. An animal to hunt, a bush I
   can pick, a stranger, anything.
3. Let me do something about food. Strawberries and hips are spoken of but I could never gather. If gathering exists,
   show me how the first time.
4. Make the hut a thing: go in, shelter, find something, or at least a line about whose it is.
5. Make the view follow the men, or say that Tab does. Keep the icons somewhere fixed so a click cannot miss.
6. Show the controls once at the start: drag to choose, right-click to send.
7. Say what "Set the packs down" is for, and say when and why it goes away.
8. Slow the move between places so it can be read, and do not drop me into a new place at nightfall.
9. Mark the season turning: a line from a man, or words on screen. And say what hunger does once the bread is gone.
10. Vary or ration the repeated lines (the birds and bushes, "No way through there", the light going), and thin the
    "hm" on every order.
