A caveat first. My view of pictures 0001 to 0053 was dropped during the run, so for the first 9 minutes I rely on the
sound and speech log plus what I did. Where I cannot quote the screen from those pictures I say so.

1. WHAT I THINK THE GAME IS, AND WHERE I WOULD HAVE QUIT

I think I lead 20 riflemen out of a mountain village on a long walk "out over the ridge, and home by spring" (Yordan,
0:11), through one place after another (village edge, beech valley, deep fir woods, gorge). I keep them fed by hunting
and gathering, camp at night, and avoid Ottoman police ("Zaptiye") and wolves. The seasons turn as I go. It is mostly
a walking game with an RTS mouse: drag a box, right click.

I do not know who the men are, why they left, or what is over the ridge. Nothing I saw told me.

Where I would have quit:
- 0:16 to 1:10, the first minute. My first right click on "The way on" made the men hide their packs under a tree
  instead of walking. I then spent a minute clicking dark patches to get them back. A real player loses their food
  here without knowing it.
- About 5 to 8 minutes in. The village edge took 10 minutes of clock to cross, with one deer and nothing else.
- 23:30 to 25:50 in the fir woods. The "way on" ring was gone from the screen and I walked south-east on a guess.

3. DECISIONS I MADE AND THINGS THAT APPEARED

- Icons under the men (fire, pack, hand, small loaves). Hovering gives "Make camp", "Close the packs" (when open) and
  "Halt"; the loaves gave "No food left" when grey. Understood. The loaves as a food gauge worked well: 4 at the
  village edge (0054), 2 on arrival in the beech valley (0057), 1 (0069), grey (0097), 4 after the kill (0102), 2 at
  the end (0129).
- Pack panel. Understood the text. Did not understand that walking past a tree would trigger it (see section 7).
- Make camp. Understood, and it works with one click. The men lie round a fire (0060) and I wait about 90 seconds of
  clock. There is nothing to do or decide at night.
- Hunting. The pointer turning into a sight, then a knife, plus the two lines about deer, taught me this fully. This
  is the best-taught thing in the game.
- Gathering. The pointer became a basket three times (6:24, 6:34, 6:50) and Boris said "Strawberries!", but I was busy
  with the deer and never gathered once. Later I hovered many bushes in the beech valley (0063 to 0069, 0075 to 0078)
  and none gave a basket. I only got refusals when a walk order happened to land on a bush: "The birds have had the
  best of this one. There will be better bushes." I never learned which bushes are food.
- Zaptiye. I took the advice and went round. Nothing was shown, nothing happened, and the music did not change. I
  cannot tell if I avoided a danger or if there was none.
- Wolves. Told twice I was followed. I saw small grey shapes behind me (0112, 0118). They never attacked. I camped
  every night, so I cannot say what happens without a fire.
- A stone hut. In the fir woods hovering it gave a flame pointer, "camp here" (25:21, 0116). In the beech valley the
  same-looking hut (0074 to 0081) gave nothing when I hovered it. I never used one.
- An axe pointer, "fell it", over trees in the gorge only (26:32, 28:24). No idea why I would fell a tree. No man
  mentioned it.
- "A hide, for shoes and coats." I do not know if I must do something with it.

10. TEN CHANGES, IN ORDER OF IMPORTANCE

1. Stop walk orders from hiding the packs. Hiding should need a deliberate act (the pack panel open, or a click on the
tree itself with a clear pointer), and right clicking the heap must lift it, not repeat the hiding.
2. Keep "The way on" findable when off screen: a mark at the screen edge pointing to it.
3. Say what the trip is at the start: who these men are, where they are going, why. Two or three lines would do.
4. Make food findable. Mark bushes that can be picked now, have a man call out animals with a direction, and give the
beech valley something to eat. I crossed it and found nothing.
5. Cut "They are away. We came on them too fast." to once per herd, never from sleeping men, and say where the animals
were.
6. Shorten or fill the village edge. Ten minutes for the first place with one deer is where people stop.
7. Warn before leaving a place that the walk costs hours, and do not land the player in the dark. Or show the place
name and hour on arrival.
8. Lift the darkness a little before the fire is lit, or outline the men, so they can be found (0086).
9. Fill the music gaps after the day track ends, and fix the wary track taking over the camp fire and the "danger
over" stroke on a hunting shot.
10. Keep the men selected after camp and on arrival, and show the camp ring and the foot-of-screen lines if they are
meant to be there. I saw neither.
