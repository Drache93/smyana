# Playtest report 5: first-time player, 21:04 of game clock, 118 steps

Played by an agent on 2026-11-23 (late night) on the build with D-151 to D-156 (men leave their block before an order, the sleeping night run faster, quiet between tunes, the menu's track, strawberries by the start, the dog rose's line, the new summer day and dusk). The step budget ran out before 30 minutes. The game never crashed. No `deaf:` or `(unstick: ...)` lines appeared at any point.

## 1 What I think it is and when I would have quit

Twenty men with long guns leave a mountain village and walk east through forest. Angel says at 0:14: "A long road. Out over the ridge, and home by spring." You select them, right-click to walk, make camp at dusk, and lose one loaf each night. The seasons turn as you go. I take it to be a journey game about getting men out and home alive before the bread runs out.

It is very pretty: the dusk light, the fire with men lying round it, the autumn colours, the rain.

In 21 minutes nothing happened to me except walking and sleeping. No animal, no stranger, no choice, and no food after the first bush. I would have quit at one of two points:
- **About 2:00 to 3:15**, when four orders in a row got "No way through there".
- **About 13:00 to 15:00**, the second day in the beech valley, when I saw that the game was right-click east, scroll, right-click east.

## 2 The first five minutes, minute by minute

- **0:00 to 0:05.** Menu. The first click on Start landed.
- **0:05 to 1:00.** Men stand in a clearing. Georgi says "Strawberries!" and Todor says "Which way? The lane runs east, down to the beeches."
  - A drag selected all of them first time. Three round icons and four loaves appeared under them, with a line at the foot of the screen: "Right click to send them".
  - The pointer turned to a basket over the red-dotted bush. I right-clicked it at 0:27 and the men walked over; gather sounds ran until 0:51. The loaves stayed at four (0010).
  - At 0:55 I right-clicked the far right edge: an "ack", then at 0:58 Yordan said "No way through there."
- **1:00 to 2:00.** Walked east in short hops. Arrow keys scroll the view.
  - At 1:26 I right-clicked an orange-berried bush. Kostadin: "Hips, but hard and green inside yet. Come the autumn." I liked that.
- **2:00 to 3:00.** Stuck (0019, 0020). Three orders, each answered with "ack" and then "No way through there" three seconds later (2:09, 2:24, 2:39). I clicked Halt at 2:56.
- **3:00 to 4:00.** Refused once more at 3:09. Went south-east at 3:18 and that worked.
  - At 3:55 Stoyan said "The light is going. We should make camp." I clicked the fire icon at 3:58.
- **4:00 to 5:00.** Fire lit at 4:10, it grew dark, crickets came up, and the men's humming began at 4:53. The best minute of the session.

## 3 Controls found / never understood

**Found**
- Drag to select, right-click to send.
- Arrow keys scroll, and they are fast: 0.6 seconds is about a screen.
- Space pauses and shows "Paused".
- The fire icon makes camp. The same slot shows a different icon in the morning; I clicked it once at 6:35 and the fire sound faded two seconds later, so I think it puts the fire out.
- The pack icon sets the packs down (9:56).
- The hand icon is Halt.
- A small ring at the screen edge shows where the men are when they are off screen. Clicking it jumps the view to them.
- The view also scrolls when the pointer sits at the screen edge. I found this by accident at 18:20: a drag that ended at the right edge slid the view a full screen away from the men (0100).

**Never understood**
- What the packs are for and why I would set them down. I picked them up again by accident at 12:04 when the pointer showed "a hand (pick these up)". I could easily have walked off without them.
- What the loaves count and what happens at zero. Hovering over them showed nothing (0007).
- Whether a fire left burning matters. I walked away from two.
- Whether the men can use their guns. Nothing to shoot ever appeared.
- Clicking the edge ring deselects the men (0114), so I had to drag again.
- The icon row floats under the men and moves with them. Twice I clicked where it had been and missed (16:12 Make camp, 21:03 Halt), and a miss deselects everybody. I was aiming from a frozen picture, so a live player would miss less often.

## 4 Lost or stuck (with clock times and picture numbers)

- **2:06 to 3:18, village edge (0018 to 0023).** Four "No way through there" answers. Looking again at 0020, low grey rock ledges cross the meadow to the north and east. I did not read them as walls at the time: the grass beyond looks the same, and a berry bush sits just past the ledge.
- **15:43 to 16:04, beech valley (0088, 0089).** Ack, then the men stood still for about 20 seconds with no words. Details in section 8.
- **16:12 to 17:16 (0094, 0095).** I thought I had made camp, but the click missed the moving icon. The men stood in near-black for a minute and nothing told me there was no camp. Night music played, with no fire sound.
- **18:20 to 18:32 (0100 to 0104).** Lost the men after the edge-scroll accident. Found them with the ring and the arrow keys.
- **19:33 to 20:25 (0109 to 0114).** I scrolled well ahead of the men and waited two steps wondering if they were stuck. They were walking; they are much slower than the camera.
- **The lane.** A pale green strip in the open, but it goes under tree crowns and I lost it often. After that I just went east.

## 5 Pace

- A day is about 2.5 minutes of walking light, then about 2 minutes of night.
- Three days and three nights passed in 21 minutes, and in every one the only thing I did was walk.
- The first place took 9 minutes, of which I wasted over a minute at the ledges and 2.5 minutes in camp.
- Crossing into the beech valley (9:12 to 9:15) threw away the afternoon. The clock read 13h at 9:00 and 18h at 9:30, and at 9:19, four seconds after arriving, Nikola said "The light is going. We should make camp." I had about 40 seconds of daylight in the new place.
- Summer became autumn at 10:00, on the second evening, and the trees were orange by morning (0065). Handsome, but abrupt.
- Nothing ever threatened me or asked me a question, so the walking felt slow even though the days are short.

## 9 Ten changes, most important first

1. Put something on the road in the first ten minutes: a track, a sound in the trees, a stranger, a choice. Three days of pure walking is where I would stop.
2. Make gathering show its result: a loaf or part-loaf appearing, or a man saying what it bought. Give the loaves a hover line and have a man speak when bread is low.
3. Make the autumn bushes give what the summer line promised, or change the line. Show the basket pointer on anything pickable, and give a spoken reason on any bush that is not.
4. When a way is blocked, do not answer "hm" first. Make the ledges at the village edge read as walls, or let the men find the way round.
5. Fix the gather sound flood (0:27 to 0:51).
6. Do not skip the afternoon on arriving in a new place, or do not call for camp four seconds after arrival.
7. Keep the order icons in a fixed place on screen, or stop a missed click from deselecting everyone. Confirm "camp made" clearly and say something if men stand in the dark with no fire.
8. Music: do not restart the day tune only to cut it 30 seconds later. Either stay quiet until dusk or play a different piece.
9. Let the player hurry the night after the first one, and say in the morning what it cost.
10. Stop a drag that ends at the screen edge from scrolling the view away, and keep the men selected when the edge ring is clicked. A place name on screen at arrival and a hint of how far there is to go would also help.
