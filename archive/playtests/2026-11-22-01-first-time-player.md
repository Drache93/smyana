# Playtest 1: first-time player report

Played 101 steps, game clock 0:00 to 18:51 (three in-game days and nights). I never left the first place, never moved more than one man at a time, never saw a goal, a word of text, a danger, or a death.

## 1. In one paragraph

I think this is a game about leading a band of about 20 riflemen in old Balkan dress on a journey out from a village through a forest, with food (loaves), camp fires and nights to manage. I am guessing most of that from the title screen, three icons and the day/night cycle; the game itself told me nothing. I do not know the goal. My best guess is "walk the band somewhere along the track", but I could not find any way to make the band walk, so for 18 minutes the 20 men stood in a column on the path while I walked single men about. I would have closed the game at about 3:00 on the game clock (roughly 8 to 10 real minutes in, because an invisible pause ate several minutes). I am fairly sure about "band on a journey"; I am not sure at all about what I was supposed to do.

## 2. The first five minutes, minute by minute

- **0:00 to 0:10 (menu).** A lovely forest scene with a column of men and the title "Смяна". No "press a key", no buttons. I clicked the scene; only then did pale "Start" and "Quit" appear over the picture, small and easy to miss. My first click on Start (0:03) did not start the game; the second (about 0:08) did. Music 01_summer_day began at 0:05, on the menu, not track 24_menu.
- **0:10 to 0:30.** Play screen. No HUD at all, no text, no objective, no pointer hint. The men, who were walking during the menu, stopped and stood still. Nothing moved for 8 seconds. I clicked a man: a ring, the name "Spas", three small icons (fire, pack, hand) and four loaves appeared. Hovering an icon gives no tooltip. **Select: understood at about 0:20.**
- **0:30.** Right click on the ground: Spas walked there. **Move: understood at 0:30**, first try.
- **0:30 to about 0:50.** Tried to move the band: drag box three times (nothing, no box drawn, and it drops the selection), double click (selects one man), Tab, right click with nothing selected. Nothing. Pressed **space**. Nothing visible happened, but it paused the game with no sign of any kind (no "Paused", no dimming; camera, selecting and icons still worked). I then spent what would be about a minute and a half of real time giving orders to men who would not move, sure the game was broken. Pressing space again by luck released it and all the queued orders ran at once.
- **About 0:50 to 1:30.** Clicked the pack icon on Mihail: the pack icon vanished, his loaves went 4 to 3 and a small brown object was left on the ground. I think he dropped his pack; I do not know why I would want that. Clicked the hand icon: it dims or brightens, nothing else. Found that **a/d/w/s scroll the camera** by pressing `a`. Rain started at about 1:10 and got heavy; it looks good.
- **1:30 to 2:30.** Clicked the fire icon on Angel: he walked off to a small clearing below the track and stood there. No fire ever appeared, in rain or later in dry weather. Sent Spas to a red berry bush: he stood by it; nothing happened.
- **2:30 to 3:00.** Tried c, x, Return, f, g, Escape (Escape only deselects; there is no pause menu or help). Sent Spas far along the track to see if the band follows a leader. Nobody follows. This is where I would have quit.
- **3:00 to 5:00.** Pure waiting and poking. The light went warm at about 4:00 (17h), then the screen was close to black by 5:00 (20h).
- **First understood any goal: never.**

## 3. Controls I found, and how

- Left click a man: select (trying it). Shows name, three icons, loaves.
- Right click ground: move that one man (trying it).
- w/a/s/d: scroll the camera (pressing `a`). Very fast when held: more than a screen a second.
- Space: pause and unpause, with no indication (found by accident; it cost me minutes).
- Escape or clicking ground: deselect.
- Pack icon: seems to drop the man's pack. Hand icon: a toggle whose effect I never saw. Fire icon: the man walks to a nearby open spot and then nothing.
- When the selected man is off-screen his icons stick to the screen edge on his side. That is a useful direction hint, but I only noticed it at 14:00.
- Suspected but never found: any way to select or move several men or the whole band (drag box, double click, Tab, Return, c, x, f, g, right click with no selection all did nothing); how to light a fire; how to pick berries; how to eat; how to shoot; any map; any help screen.

## 4. Where I was lost or stuck

- **0:00, menu.** Nothing on screen says how to start. A visible "Start" from the first frame would fix it.
- **0:10, first play screen.** Zero guidance. One line of words over a man's head ("Which way, voivoda?" or whatever fits) or a pulsing marker down the track would have told me what the next minute is for.
- **About 0:45 to 0:55, invisible pause.** The worst moment of the session. The screen looked live and men ignored orders. A "Paused" label would fix it.
- **0:30 to the end, moving the band.** Drag select does nothing. This is the one thing every player of a top-down game with 20 men will try first. I never got past it, so I never saw the rest of the game.
- **2:00, 4:20, 15:03, fire.** The fire icon makes a man walk a few steps and stop. No fire, no "need wood", no cursor change asking me to pick a spot.
- **1:00, 7:10, 9:30, berries.** Bright red bushes are the only thing in the forest that looks like it can be used. Right clicking them and using the hand icon next to them does nothing I can see.
- **8:00.** Spas stopped dead at a low rock step and stood for 25 seconds with a move order beyond it. No "can't get there" sign.
- **11:00 and 14:00 to 14:30.** Lost my man in the dark, then spent five steps (30 to 40 real seconds) scrolling through look-alike forest to find my own band. No minimap, no "jump to the band" key.

## 5. Pace

- An in-game hour is about 15 to 20 seconds; a whole day and night is about six and a half minutes. Night arrives suddenly: warm tint at about 4:00, nearly black at 5:00.
- Because I could not move the band, **nothing at all happened for 18 minutes** apart from rain at 1:10 and the light changing. The longest fully dead stretches: 3:00 to 5:00 (2 minutes), each night (5:00 to 6:30, 11:20 to 12:40, 17:30 to 18:51, about 90 seconds each of a black screen with nothing to do), and 15:03 to 18:51 (nearly 4 minutes).
- Nothing came too fast. I was never rushed and never under any pressure. Standing still for three days cost me nothing that I could see, which also means the game gave me no push to work out how to move.
- Real-time note: with the hidden pause, the first "minute" of game clock was about three minutes for a real player.

## 6. Sound and music as told to me

Every cue in the log:

- 0:05 01_summer_day begins (on the menu). Fits the bright morning scene. No separate menu music.
- 0:52 "the music ends" and 01_summer_day begins again. This was during or just after my accidental pause; the track seems to have run on while the game was frozen and then restarted.
- 2:49 ends, 2:51 01_summer_day again. Fits (14h, daylight), but it is the third start of the same tune in three minutes.
- 7:03 ends, 7:05 01_summer_day (dawn of day 2). So by the log the same track or silence covered the whole first dusk and night (4:00 to 6:30). Nothing marked the first nightfall, which is the single biggest change on screen in the first ten minutes.
- 11:17 ends, 11:19 **02_summer_dusk** begins. First new track, 11 minutes in. It arrived at 20h, when the screen was already nearly black; the warm dusk light it describes had been and gone a minute earlier (about 10:20).
- 14:37 ends, 14:39 01_summer_day (midday, day 3). Fits.
- 18:51 ends, in the middle of the third night. No dusk track on the third evening at all, though the second evening had one.

Other points:
- **Not one sound effect in 18 minutes.** No shot, no wolf, no footstep, no rain (heavy rain from 1:10 with, by the key, no sound). Clicking icons, dropping a pack, giving orders: silent.
- **Nobody said a word** in 18 minutes. No "said:" line at all.
- The music is on a fixed rotation that does not follow what is on screen. Night had day music or nothing; dusk music came after dusk. If the dusk track started as the light turned warm, it would tell me "evening, think about camp" a full minute before the dark does, which the picture alone does not.
- 01_summer_day started five times in 18 minutes. It would wear thin.
- I cannot tell from the log where the silences were, because some gaps between "begins" lines are 2 minutes and some 4. If the track is about 2 minutes, then about 4:50 to 7:03, 9:05 to 11:17 and 16:40 to 18:51 were silent: two minutes or more each, twice over a black screen.

## 7. What I enjoyed, and what made me want to stop

Enjoyed:
- The look. The forest, the painted trees, the costumes and the rain at 1:10 to 2:10 are the best thing here. The title screen made me want to play.
- Trees fading out when a man walks behind them.
- Finding the berry glade at 9:30 and the river at 11:00. For a moment it felt like a place worth exploring.
- The men have names.

Wanted to stop:
- 0:45: orders ignored (the hidden pause).
- 1:30: third failed drag box. Twenty men and I can move one.
- 3:00: no goal, no text, no sound, nothing reacting. This is where I would have closed it.
- 9:30: found berries and could not do anything with them.
- 12:30: a man spent a night alone in the forest and nothing happened, so the night has no teeth.

It did not get better by pushing on. The world got bigger (glade, river, meadows) but nothing in it did anything.

## 8. Reasons to explore; hunger, cold, night, danger

- **A reason to leave the path?** No. I left it only because I could not do anything else. The berry bushes look like a reason, but they give nothing. The river can be waded with no effect.
- **Hunger.** Not understood. Loaves by the icons suggest food. Spas went 4 to 3 once in the first minute and stayed at 3 for three days. Nobody looked hungry or said so. I do not know if men eat, when, or what zero means.
- **Cold.** Nothing on screen. Heavy rain and three nights without a fire had no visible effect.
- **Night.** I can see it is night (the screen is nearly black, too dark to find a man). I do not know what night means for the men. Angel lay down on the first night; the band stood in column all night. No consequence.
- **Danger.** None seen or heard in 18 minutes. The men carry rifles and I never learned why.

## 9. Ten changes that would most help a first-time player

1. Make the band move as one by default, or make drag-box selection work, because nothing else in the game is reachable until this is.
2. Put a goal on screen in the first ten seconds: a spoken line over the leader's head and a visible marker or direction down the track.
3. Show "Paused" when space is pressed.
4. Show Start and Quit on the title screen from the first frame, and take the first click.
5. Give the three icons a one-word tooltip on hover and a visible result when pressed (a fire appears, or a line says why it cannot).
6. Make berry bushes do something on right click, with a word or a loaf appearing, since they are the only thing that invites a click.
7. Have the men speak in the first minutes ("We should move before the rain", "It is getting dark, make camp") so the world explains hunger, night and fire.
8. Tie music to the light: dusk track when the tint turns warm, night track at dark, and do not restart the day tune every two minutes.
9. Add any sound for rain, clicks and orders, and one early far-off wolf or shot so the player knows the forest is not safe.
10. Add a key that jumps the camera to the band, slow the keyboard scroll, and keep the night bright enough to see the men.
