# Playtest report 7: first-time player, 8:45 of game clock, 45 steps (cut short)

Played by an agent on 2026-11-24 on the build with D-173 and D-174. The run stopped early: the session that played it
was paused for an hour by a usage limit, and when it came back the frozen game was gone (its log ends in plain tick
lines with no error, so whether it crashed or was cleared away while it sat frozen is not known). What follows is the
agent's report, shortened only where it repeats itself.

## What it thinks the game is, and where it would have quit

Twenty men with flintlock rifles leave a stone mountain village in summer to walk "out over the ridge, and home by
spring". You drag to select them and right-click to send them. They carry four days of bread, can shoot game, and must
camp at night by a fire. "It looks like a slow, pretty survival march through seasons. Nothing told me why they are
going."

It would have quit:

- About 2:30 to 3:00. "The men walked a long way round and then stood in a clump at the foot of a cliff, in a black
  shadow, saying nothing. I had clicked on ground on top of the cliff. A real player sees men who have stopped
  obeying."
- 8:30 to 8:45. "After eight and a half minutes of walking 'east' I reached a hard black diagonal edge of the world.
  The men lined up along it and nothing happened: no words, no transition, no hint. This is the most likely quitting
  point."
- "The whole first day was nothing but walking with the view not following the men, so every order needed Tab or the
  arrow keys first."

## Decisions, and things that appeared

"Real decisions: only two." To shoot the deer or not: "I understood the cursor but not Mihail's advice: 'come up slow
and stop short' names nothing I can do. I never learnt whether we hit." And when and where to camp: clear and well
timed.

- Rose hips: "A nice touch the first time. There are a great many of them, and a second man said the same line at
  6:36."
- Roe deer: "I never saw anything I recognised as a deer ... a small brown upright mark at the bottom of the screen
  that I took for a fence post. Mihail's line appeared while the men were at the screen edge."
- Rain, the stream, far wolves: each noticed, each liked. "The night was the best part."

## Finding the way

"I was told 'east' three times, but nothing on screen says which way east is. The view is tilted, and the lanes run
diagonally down-right, straight right and down. I guessed screen-right. That took me to the cliff ... I then followed
the lane down-right into a dark wood I took for 'the beeches', and met the black edge. I never knew whether I was at
the right spot on the edge ... A compass mark or a man pointing would fix it."

(The build it played showed the "way on" ring only from the second place. It never got there.)

## What confused it

- The view does not follow the men.
- The signs travel with the men; once they sat far from the men, by the spot that had been clicked on the cliff.
- After "Make camp" the signs vanished and the men were deselected.
- "Set the packs down": never learnt what it is for.
- A game day is about five minutes, so four days of food is twenty minutes, and the one hunt gave no result.
- Selection rings thin and pale on daylight grass. Speech pale, in the middle of the screen, fading.
- "Nothing said the shot missed or hit, that the cliff could not be climbed, or that I had reached the edge."

## Sound and music

Menu music, the summer day track, rain, stream, wading, crickets, fire, owl and far howls each came in with the
picture. The volley and the 18 seconds of reloading: convincing. Two orders gave no grunt (the eight-second rule).
Silences: 2:05 to 3:54, and from 8:15 to the end.

## Bugs and oddities

- Men ordered onto ground above a cliff walk to its foot and stop, with no word.
- "Large flat black shadow shapes with hard straight diagonal edges and oval holes in them, under the trees by the
  cliff. It looks like a drawing fault, not shade."
- A big tree's leaves appear and vanish as the men pass.
- Men under the canopy at the screen edge are nearly invisible. The deer reads as a post.
- At night the men all stand with rifles shouldered round a very small glow; it does not read as a camp.
- "The world ends in a razor-straight black diagonal. Tab no longer centres the men there."
- The hips line repeats word for word.

## The start

"The menu is the village itself with the title in Cyrillic and the men milling about. It is handsome and I understood
it. 'Start' and 'Quit' are small, pale and lie over the scene ... There is no word of what the game is or why the men
leave, and no Latin-letter title."

## Its ten changes

1. Make the way out of the first place unmistakable; never leave the men lined up against a black void in silence.
2. Show which way east is.
3. Make the view follow the selected men.
4. When an order cannot be reached, have a man say so.
5. Give the hunt an outcome, and make the deer look like a deer.
6. Fix the signs to one place on the screen.
7. Shorten the first place or put more in it; state the goal and the reason in the first minute.
8. Say what camp did in the morning; explain "Set the packs down".
9. Make speech easier to read; stop repeated lines.
10. Tidy the hard black shadow shapes and the world's edge, the popping canopy, the selection rings, men who sit by
    the fire.

Got to: the village edge, 8:45, summer, second day; 20 men, three loaves; 45 steps.
