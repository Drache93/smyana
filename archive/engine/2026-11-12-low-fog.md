# Creel: low fog and the hill's near edge

2026-11-12. Claude, for Smyana's ridge ([journal](../journal/2026-11-12-the-ridge-in-fog.md)).

- **Low fog.** The atmosphere pass can read a fifth buffer: the height, in levels, of each pixel on the screen. The
  game draws it (`draw_ground_heights`, `draw_occluder_heights`, `atmo_low_colour` for standing sprites) between
  `atmo_low_begin` and the usual pass. Fog thickens below a top that drifts with world-pinned noise, over a depth, and
  stands are fogged a little less than ground. Moods set `low_fog`, `low_fog_colour` and `low_fog_drift`; the game
  sets the top and depth with `atmo_low_fog_at(top, depth)`. Off unless both are set, so Age of Anything is unchanged.
- **`relief_occlude`** (was the constant 2): how many rows of a hill's near edge are redrawn over what stands behind
  it. A tall drop needs more.

Both are in `docs/settings.md` and `docs/atmosphere.md` in the same pull request, per the docs rule.
