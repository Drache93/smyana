# Guns: a model held along the hand, sounds loaded and panned (2026-11-12)

Creel pull request [Casaide/creel#28](https://github.com/Casaide/creel/pull/28), for Smyana's flintlock (#58,
[journal](../journal/2026-11-12-the-flintlock.md)). Made by Claude.

- **A held model along the hand** (`view/voxel`). A gear model's x was always the hold's direction, so a long gun's
  barrel could never point where the hand points. A shape can now say `"side"`, its x that way.
- **Sounds** (`game/audio`). Creel played sounds from a list nothing filled since it left Age of Anything.
  `sounds_load()` reads the game's `data/audio.json`; `play_sound_at` pans a sound left or right.
- Both written into Creel's `docs/settings.md`, as the docs rule asks.

Found and left: a model's thin parts (one voxel) drop out when it's baked at an angle. Smyana covers its barrel with a
capsule; Creel's docs say so.
