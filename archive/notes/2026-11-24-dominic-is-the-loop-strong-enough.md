# Dominic, on the first-time plays and the loop, 2026-11-24

Written in the project's chat at 14:06 (Bulgarian time). His words, unchanged:

> those tests games getting shorter? are they actually playing much or mostly walking and surviving? how are they on clarity of what there is to do? feels too easy to just walk forward on the first map, that said i then came across a deer which meant things started to get interesting. is our gameplay loop strong enough?

Claude's answer, in short: the plays get further, not shorter (2, 3, then 4 places in half an hour), and that is the
trouble. About 17 of the ninth play's 29 minutes were plain walking, 7 were nights, 3 were hunting; a real choice came
about every eight to ten minutes; no man has died in any first-time play and an empty pack cost nothing anyone saw.
Proposed: hunger that shows, the first map's food found in its first three minutes with the caller pointing, danger
that shows itself, and one thing for home in each place with its empty place in the pack.
