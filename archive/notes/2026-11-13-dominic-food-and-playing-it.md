## What Claude took from it

- Keep the four days' food; build hunting with the rifles, and make foraging findable.
- The men must always show, whatever stands in front of them.
- Show what can be clicked and what a right-click will do.
- Columns on a march; music that keeps playing.
- The wait between places was the letter's twenty seconds, not the load.
- The big one: players need to know what to do and why. Proposal in `docs/clarity.md`.
