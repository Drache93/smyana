# Timeline

One line per moment worth remembering. Newest at the bottom.

- **2026-11-08:** First voice memo: "everything about the art comes from the trees, the leaves,
  the ferns". ([audio](audio/2026-11-10/04-art-comes-from-the-trees.ogg))
- **2026-11-10:** Three more voice memos lay out the game: twenty villagers, the forest, the
  seasons, caches, the race home. ([transcripts](notes/2026-11-10-voice-memo-transcripts.md))
- **2026-11-10:** The Bulgarian man, the first art test for the game: a concept sheet, then a
  voxel model in three cloaks. ([image](images/2026-11-10/02-bulgarian-man-concept-sheet.png))
- **2026-11-10:** First design doc and a flat mock-up. Rejected: it didn't look like the engine.
- **2026-11-10:** Mock-ups redone as paintovers of real Age of Anything frames: late summer,
  autumn dusk, first snow. ([image](images/2026-11-10/16-mock-autumn-dusk-fire.jpg))
- **2026-11-10:** The Kovachevitsa builders become the heart of the story.
- **2026-11-10:** No jobs: it's a survival RPG built as an RTS.
- **2026-11-10:** Camera goes under the canopy. ([image](images/2026-11-10/18-mock-under-the-canopy.jpg))
- **2026-11-10:** Builder sprites: three outfits, four facings. ([image](images/2026-11-10/14-builder-sprites.png))
- **2026-11-10:** "The route": five rendered frames through different woods. Dominic: "amazing".
  ([image](images/2026-11-10/22-route-roman-ruins.jpg))
- **2026-11-10:** Named **Смяна (Smyana)**. Public repo and archive started.
- **2026-11-10:** Decided the archive keeps all of Dominic's voice notes and messages word for
  word, to show the human direction behind an AI-made game.
  ([his words](notes/2026-11-10-dominic-in-his-own-words.md))
- **2026-11-10:** The men's looks to mix at random (5 hats, 3 coats, 6 cloaks, an older face), the
  swallow-tail rifle and the masons' tools. ([image](images/2026-11-10/30-men-random-mix.png))
- **2026-11-10:** The environment broken into GitHub issues (#3, with 15 sub-issues), including one
  tracking Creel's lighting work (Casaide/creel#5). Each place on the route is its own map, and
  the UI stays as close to none as possible. ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-environment-issues.md))
- **2026-11-10:** Environmental obstacles added: fixing a bridge, fording a river, and a landslide
  found after the fact (#20). ([his words](notes/2026-11-10-dominic-in-his-own-words.md#breaking-the-game-into-issues))
- **2026-11-10:** The first runnable version: Smyana as its own program on Creel, two grey-boxed
  places, twenty men who can be ordered about, and the walk out and home again with a letter
  between places (#4). ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-first-runnable-version.md))
- **2026-11-10:** The trees brought down to the mock-ups' size, with tree size set per place
  (whole trees, or trunks fading to the sky), the Rhodopes' own kinds, a rich autumn, and
  gneiss outcrops. ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-rhodope-trees-ground-and-rock.md))
- **2026-11-10:** The light worked toward the target picture: a low sun with long shadows, one
  deep shade, rays and mist in the air, darker woods round a lit clearing, and a campfire.
  ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-lighting-and-mood.md), [engine](engine/2026-11-10-sun-and-shade.md))
- **2026-11-10:** A first try at the music: "Autumn dusk", a slow kaval tune over a kaba gaida
  drone, written as text and turned into sound by code, with notes on the style of Rhodope
  music. ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-first-music-spike.md),
  [listen](audio/2026-11-10/05-music-spike-autumn-dusk.ogg))
- **2026-11-10:** "A little too Viking... Needs more mist": a second, hazier take, and Dominic's
  rule that the music follows the game and ends as fast as it began (D-036), with a demo going
  from calm to hiding to a fight to silence. ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-first-music-spike.md#later-the-same-evening-more-mist-and-music-that-follows-danger))
- **2026-11-10:** The game will swap whole music tracks (D-042). A proposed list of 24 tracks,
  and five more sketches trying tambura, gadulka, bells and two-part singing: a fight, hiding,
  morning, autumn dusk again, the ruins. ([track list](../docs/music-tracks.md),
  [journal](journal/2026-11-10-first-music-spike.md#later-still-a-track-list-and-the-other-instruments))
- **2026-11-10:** The ruins made "more mystic and ancient", a first sung line without words,
  and the track list put up as issue #26 under a new audio label.
  ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-first-music-spike.md#last-of-the-evening-older-ruins-singing-and-an-issue))
- **2026-11-10:** Fallen leaves you can see on the ground, herbs in drifts, and the landslide moved to a
  ledge between a crag and a real cliff (Creel's high ground, first use; a gneiss cliff face).
  ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-rhodope-trees-ground-and-rock.md#ground-you-can-read-and-a-landslide-on-a-ledge-2210-to-2240))
- **2026-11-10:** Lint for the Lobster code: `lobsterfmt`'s layout and no warnings, in `./check.sh` (D-044).
  ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-lint-and-lobsterfmt.md))
- **2026-11-10:** The camp as voxel models: a canvas tent, a lean-to of striped rugs, the fireplace with its tripod,
  a woodpile, four riding horses and four pack mules (D-045).
  ([journal](journal/2026-11-10-the-camp-tents-fire-and-horses.md))
