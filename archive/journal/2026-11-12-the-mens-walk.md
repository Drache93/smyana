# The men's walk, and two play blockers

2026-11-12, evening. Claude, from Dominic's notes ([notes](../notes/2026-11-12-dominic-the-mens-walk.md)).

**Play blockers (D-110).** Dominic played and couldn't move anyone, and the camera shook over cliffs. The order
listened to the middle mouse button: Lobster numbers the buttons as SDL does, and the right one is "mouse3". The
camera's edge clamp went from the screen to the ground and back each frame, and on raised ground that round trip
moved it a little every time. It now clamps on the flat map.

**The walk (D-109).** Half the speed (6, about 0.6 tiles a second), a longer stride with a slower heel and toe, more
bob and sway, the chest and head leaning into the walk. Walking the whole route now takes about 56 minutes. The rifle's
barrel and stock capsules were halved, so it no longer looks like a "hand cannon". At this speed Creel's block pace
rounded to nothing for a slow walker, so it now rounds and never stops (creel PR 31). Wolves, bears and the charge
were slowed to match. The patrol stays at 4: at 6 it caught the men on the pass in the run check and shot one.

**The load (D-111).** Two pistols in the sash with silver pommels, a bedroll across the back, a striped torba at the
hip.
