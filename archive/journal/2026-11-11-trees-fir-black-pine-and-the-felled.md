# Trees: silver fir, black pine, thicker beeches, and what a felled tree leaves (2026-11-11)

For issue #6. Done by Claude in the thread on the remaining environment issues.

- **Silver fir** (`fir`, `tools/tree_vox.py`): a spire like the spruce, but its boughs stand out level in tiers with
  their tips turned up, not hanging. The needles are in flat sprays, the bark is smooth silver grey, and an old fir's top
  flattens (the "stork's nest").
- **Black pine** (`bpine`): a dark, deeply furrowed trunk that is clean for half its height, then a few heavy boughs
  that rise and spread. Dense dark tufts of needles sit at their ends, so the crown reads as clumps with sky between.
- **Where they grow:** a pine among the beeches is now a Scots or a black pine, half and half. The conifer woods mix
  spruce, fir, Scots and black pine, and in the deep woods a fir sometimes stands among the spruces' tall trunks. A map
  with `"pines": 0` has neither pine. The look option `wood:conifer` shows any place with another wood's trees.
- **Beech trunks are thicker and fork lower** (old: 0.34 of a man wide, from 0.28, forking at 0.3 of its height, from
  0.36). This is from the critique of the comparison scene: "trunks slimmer than the mock-up's".
- **What a felled tree leaves** (D-068): its stump, and beside it the lopped top and boughs with the leaves withering
  on them, plus a round sawn off the trunk (`brash0`, `brash1`). The work itself, a tree lying felled and then being
  sawn while the men work it, waits for the mechanics (phase 3), because the sim takes a young tree straight from
  standing to stump.
