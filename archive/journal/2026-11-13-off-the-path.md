# Off the path: one goal, words for orders, and side paths

*2026-11-13, Claude, thread "Make the gameplay clear and add hunting".* Dominic's words are in
`archive/notes/2026-11-13-dominic-off-the-path.md`.

After playing the hunting build Dominic said the game was all about the trail: "there's no reason to do anything
else". He wants players off it, dodging patrols into the wood and meeting a bear there, or finding things, or camping
while the riders pass. He also said no place should have its own goal (one goal: home with men, resources and
knowledge), and that teaching keys was "pretty bad". The plan is `docs/off-the-path.md`.

## Words for orders (D-126)

With men chosen, a short row at the foot of the screen: *Make camp* (or *Put the fire out*, *Light the fire*), *Put the
packs down*, *Halt*, and under them *Bread for 4 days*. It sits on a soft dark band so it reads over the brightest
crowns (`images/2026-11-13/23-the-order-signs-with-the-men-chosen.jpg`).

## Seeing the men (D-127)

Dominic offered two ways: do more see-through, or thin the trees by the path. Thinning would undo the dense woods and
only help on the path, so the cut-away is wider instead (`22-the-men-under-the-beeches-before-and-after-a-wider-cutaway.jpg`).

## Side paths (D-128)

When a place's wood has grown, four paths are cut from the trail into it: a cart track first, then shepherd's paths
and deer tracks, each ending in a small clearing. Patrols look down each one they pass and turn down the bigger ones
more often. Keeping them away from the gates mattered: a cart track ending by the fir woods' gate put a clearing where
the camp check's cold men were sent, and they never got clear of the fire. The bear check had to follow the bear, which
now wandered off from where the men were sent.

Next from the plan: game deeper in the wood with signs at the trail's edge, and finds at the paths' ends.
