# Finds off the path, and a camp that shows its fire

*2026-11-13, evening. Claude, from Dominic's "Yes" to more to find on the way home and a faster winter walk, and his
playtest at 19:41.*

## What was built

- **Finds at the side paths' ends** (`src/smyana/finds.lobster`, D-131). Up to two a place by the run's seed, never two
  of a kind, at the end of a path where no game is. An **empty shepherds' hut** (a new voxel model,
  `art/camp/vox/hut.vox`: dry-stacked gneiss, a slab roof, one door; picture 24): a camp at it has its fire inside, seen
  from four tiles instead of thirty-four at night, and the men sleep out of the wind. **Another band's cache** under
  stones, twelve to twenty-two portions, not seen from the trail. **Wild bees' honey** in a hollow tree in the beech
  and oak woods. A cache and the honey are piles of their own kind, so a patrol takes them and the bear smells them
  like the men's own. Right-click a hut to camp in it.
- **The trail is quick in the snow** (D-132): snow trodden by riders slows the men 35 hundredths as much as in the wood.
- **The test bot** takes what it finds on the way home (only what it can walk to, twice at most), sleeps in a hut on
  the way home when one is near, and walks on to the gate late in the day when it is close.

## What the playthroughs found

The first run with finds crashed in a fight (a man killed that tick still listed; fixed), and showed men who camped
at ten at night and never ate: with no wood the fire took fifty seconds, six hours of the fast night clock, so it had
not caught by morning. The second run brought **2 of 20 men home**, the first time without pay: they picked up five
other bands' caches and two lots of honey on the way. Most deaths were still in the deep fir woods and the beech
valley at the end of winter, with the men sleepless after shooting at wolves all night. A third run was stopped for
Dominic's playtest.

## Dominic's playtest

"telling them to make camp, i got no fire" (picture 25): the fire was being laid and nothing showed. Now the
fireplace shows at once (D-133). His larger point, that the hover text reads as placeholder, most trees can't be
felled, and gathering needs to be made enjoyable, is a design question; Claude is bringing a proposal before more UI.
